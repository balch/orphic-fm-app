Storms, solos, and a vibe reborn.

• Weather runs through a song now: rain that falls, thunder that rolls past, lightning that cracks.
• Solos step clear of the mix, and the band builds behind them instead of holding still.
• Chord progressions that used to freeze after the first section now evolve all the way through.
• Roomier controls on phone, tablet and TV.
