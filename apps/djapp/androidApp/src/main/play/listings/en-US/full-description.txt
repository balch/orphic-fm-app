Orphic DJ is an interactive album — original generative music that composes itself, endlessly, every time you press play.

There's no streaming, no library, no playlists. The music is created live on your device, so no two listens are ever the same.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 11 original vibes — from upbeat grooves to deep ambient drones
• 11 live visualizations that move with the music
• 8 generative tracks layering every beat
• 5 mixer controls to ride the levels
• 4 ambiance knobs: Energy, Complexity, Mood, Space
• 2 turntables — dual decks with a crossfader
• 1 home-screen widget — your current vibe and playback at a glance
∞ infinite music — it never repeats

WHAT YOU CONTROL
Four knobs capture the essence of any track:
• Energy — from calm ambient drift to a driving beat
• Complexity — sparse and minimal, or rich and layered
• Mood — bright and uplifting, or dark and moody
• Space — intimate and dry, or vast and reverberant

MADE FOR EVERY MOMENT
• Focus & study — steady, distraction-free instrumental music for deep work
• Relax & sleep — wind down with a built-in timer that fades you off to sleep
• House parties — open the DJ module, layer effects, and drop changes on the fly
• Long drives & commutes — music that never repeats and never needs a signal

Always offline — no account, no network, no ads, no telemetry. Your music never leaves your device. No sign-up, no tracking, no subscription.
