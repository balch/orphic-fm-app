# v2.0.3 store assets — iPad and Android TV

Device support for both form factors already ships in the binary. What was missing is the
**store-side** assets that make each form factor visible in its store:

- Play will not surface the app on the **Android TV** form factor without a TV banner *and* at
  least one TV screenshot, no matter what `AndroidManifest.xml` declares.
- App Store Connect will not advertise **iPad** support without iPad screenshots, no matter what
  `TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY` says.

## Required dimensions

### Play — Android TV

- **Banner:** exactly **1280x720** px, PNG or JPEG, **no alpha channel**. The 640x360 in-app
  drawable is half this in each axis; upscaling it is lossy, so regenerate from source art.
- **Screenshots:** 1-8 images, **16:9**, minimum **1920x1080** (3840x2160 also accepted), PNG or
  JPEG, no alpha.

### Play — tablets

- **7-inch and 10-inch:** each side 320-3840 px, aspect between 16:9 and 9:16. Play wants at least
  4 per form factor to treat the app as large-screen optimised, so capture 4-8.

### App Store — iPad

- **iPad 13":** **2064x2752** portrait or **2752x2064** landscape. This is the only iPad size App
  Store Connect still requires; it scales down for smaller iPads.
- iPhone 6.9" (1320x2868) remains required and is unchanged by this release.

## Capture targets

Everything needed is already on this machine:

| Form factor | Target | Command |
|---|---|---|
| Android TV | `Television_4K` AVD | `emulator -avd Television_4K` |
| Tablet | `Pixel_Tablet` AVD | `emulator -avd Pixel_Tablet` |
| Phone | Samsung SM-S938U on USB | already attached |
| iPad | `iPad Pro 13-inch (M4)` simulator | `xcrun simctl boot` |

Android capture: `adb -s <serial> exec-out screencap -p > shot.png`
iOS capture: `xcrun simctl io <udid> screenshot shot.png`

**TV gotcha:** the TV launcher caches the banner, so a changed banner may not appear until the
launcher's data is cleared. A stale `.debug` build also steals audio focus from the release build —
uninstall it before evaluating audio on TV.
